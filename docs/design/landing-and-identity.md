# Landing and identity: first exploration

Working brand: **Soulfire**. Domain availability and trademark clearance are not established. This is a visual exploration, not a final identity.

## Visual direction

Warm ivory, deep charcoal, and restrained amber. Spacious layouts, expressive but readable typography, accessible contrast, and subtle motion. Aim for warmth, clarity, and steadiness. Test both phone and desktop. Spiritual depth can emerge through language and pace rather than literal religious imagery.

## Landing-page concept

Navigation: Soulfire wordmark, The practice, Sign in.

Headline: **Meet what's here. Find your next step.**

Supporting copy: **A spiritual practice companion for the things you're struggling with. Explore twelve steps through reflection, meditation, and exercises shaped around you.**

Primary interaction: **What's weighing on you today?** A text composer with editable dictation and a distinct live-voice control. Primary action: **Find a practice**. Clarify AI processing before submission and microphone processing before activation. Do not imply unsaved means unprocessed.

Optional starting points: **A pattern I can't break · Worry that won't let go · Feeling low or disconnected · Conflict with someone.** These open a conversation rather than assign a diagnosis.

Below the opening: show a representative interactive exercise, then a concise explanation of personalized practice and the twelve-step map. Use a lightweight demonstration until real generation exists; do not imply a mockup provides live AI guidance. Include accessible privacy and support information and an accurate self-help description.

Design the first phone screen before expanding desktop composition. Avoid crowding all four starting points, legal text, and twelve steps into the hero. Do not require an account to experience the initial demonstration.

## Logo directions

1. **Sheltered ember:** a simple flame or seed inside an open protective arc. Warmth and an inner spark. Ensure it does not resemble a fuel, camping, or insurance logo.
2. **Opening circle:** an incomplete circle surrounding a small spark, suggesting room to return and openness. Avoid visual detail that could read as a loading spinner.
3. **Quiet S:** an abstract S formed from two fluid strokes, with a subtle flame-like counterform. Recognizable at small sizes without requiring an explanation.

Compare black-only versions first, including at 16–32 pixels. Test app icon, navigation, light/dark backgrounds, and the Soulfire wordmark. Generated images are exploration artifacts, not production vectors or proof of uniqueness. Refine the chosen mark into vector geometry and typeset the name separately.
