Architecture decisions and migration plan · Validation evidence

What is real, and what comes next

Working now

  • Optional Kokoro intro and Play/Replay history.
  • Steps 1, 3, 10 and 11 with editable trusted blocks.
  • Private reflection, selected sharing, optional quiet and chosen action.
  • Opt-in saved practice in this browser.
  • Listening waveform, explicit Stop and Discard.
  • Music, motion controls and reduced-motion support.
  • Visual influence map with keyboard/simple controls.
  • Opt-in local memory: edit, pause AI use, delete.
  • Per-request memory consent and stale-write protection.

Proposed / deliberately deferred

  • Twelve step-specific interactive scene recipes.
  • Stable shell with Why this, adjust and simple view.
  • Shared pilot identity and per-user storage isolation.
  • Human usefulness studies and all-step scene validation.
Research and evidence boundaries

HAX and PAIR inform correction, control and recovery. CHI design principles inform adaptive interfaces. WCAG informs accessible alternatives. Laboratory work on labeling or sound does not validate soulfire as treatment.
