# First personalized practice: concept

[Desktop and mobile mockup](first-practice-sage-gold.png).

Current version uses sage consistently for the headings, all three exercise groups, icons, and primary controls, with gold confined to the flame and small accents. The [earlier version](archive/first-practice-concept-v1.png) is retained as history, not the selected direction.

Scenario: "I have a presentation tomorrow and can't stop worrying." Connect to Step One's acceptance practice, keeping agency in the next response. Copy and interactions are proposals, not final coaching instructions.

Desktop pairs a companion panel with three exercise groups: what the person can prepare, influence, and release. Mobile stacks the groups and retains a compact conversation entry and continuation controls. Suggested cards can be edited, moved through accessible controls, removed, or supplemented. End with an optional small preparation action.

Carry forward the selected sage/gold palette and sheltered-flame working mark. The reference image is a visual concept, not a functioning app. The full mobile page must include all groups and the action field beyond the visible crop. Sticky controls must not obscure fields, keyboard focus, or screen-reader navigation.

Text, editable dictation, and voice share conversation state. The displayed private-draft caption is a design goal, not an implemented privacy guarantee; explain separately when dictation or live voice transmits audio. Do not send draft exercise contents just because the person opens voice mode. Continue should explain what happens next and obtain the person's choice before sharing answers for AI reflection.

Pause, a different-practice option, and optional grounding remain accessible. Grounding is not a replacement for appropriate support. Verify readability, contrast, card movement, small-screen scrolling, and voice transitions in the eventual browser implementation.
