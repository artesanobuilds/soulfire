# ADR-005: Goal-first creation and source-mapped starter catalogue

Date: 29 October 2026. Status: approved design direction; implementation/audio production pending.

Miguel requested that Create for me begin with how a person feels, what is difficult, and what they want to change, then suggest a starter Current to shape or create a custom one. Musical parameters move into optional Advanced controls. Shape now retains its immediate DSP contract.

The exact original Markdown used by the synthetic-user work was located from its repository provenance reference, read fully, and copied unchanged into a new dated archive. Its 37,933 bytes and SHA256 match the prior record. Its 20 numbered topics include clinical conditions, populations and structural access contexts; they are not all diagnoses. The ordering and care-shortfall claims are not independently validated product priorities or efficacy evidence.

The design specifies 40 seed concepts, two per source topic, using contrasting taste profiles. Each has explicit use, suggestion rationale, evidence limit, eligibility, recipe profile, QA policy and availability state. None is generated or ready audio. Recommendation means transparent fit to explicit goals/preferences, not a clinical best match. Users can choose another style, custom creation, skip, silence or Stop.

The adolescent pair is future-design coverage only, unavailable in the adult prototype. Crisis-labelled concepts are not crisis recommendations; human support takes precedence, with optional accompaniment only. Withdrawal and other acute medical concerns likewise override listening suggestions. Identity, poverty and geography never determine a musical prescription. No demographic inference or new screening is introduced.

This decision does not replace ADR-004's model boundary: Turbo musical parameters remain approximate requests; live DSP operates on approved prepared assets. No application change, bulk generation, model download, push or deployment is authorized by this documentation revision.
