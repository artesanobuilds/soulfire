# ADR-004: evolve Current through a constrained ACE hybrid

Date: 2026-10-29. Decision owner: Miguel. Status: ACE-Step 1.5 Turbo selected for design; implementation and migration pending document review.

## Decision and evidence boundary

Use the already working, pinned ACE-Step 1.5 Turbo adapter as the candidate-music source for the proposed next Current. Combine it with explicit preference policy, measurable acoustic checks, listening review, deterministic DSP and versioned session scheduling. The supplied IsoTune report instead recommends symbolic/rule-based generation through curated instruments. That divergence is intentional and must remain visible: the chosen path favors musical richness and existing readiness, while giving up exact note-level control.

This is not proof that Turbo is the best mental-health generator. Stable was not auditioned, human preferences were not rated, and no clinical efficacy trial of Current exists. The existing small local benchmark supports feasibility and replay mechanics only.

## Two modes

Shape now: immediate bounded DSP/mixing of approved prepared audio. New instruments, harmony, melody or major tempo change create an asynchronous replacement request. No independent layer/density slider unless real compatible stems exist.

Create for me: queued multi-candidate generation, measurement, rejection/listening review, transition assembly and a named saved session. It offers more selection time, not guaranteed effectiveness. Both use one artifact policy, manifest and player.

## Alternatives preserved

- Procedural Current v1: retain source, tests and saved recipes; tested fallback and evaluation baseline.
- Symbolic/rule-based composition: retain as the stronger exact-control alternative if note-level guarantees become decisive or ACE acceptance rates are poor.
- Stable Audio: retain Music Lab source/docs, pending license/profile gate and incomplete comparison; no acceptance or download is implied.
- Other/paid APIs and larger ACE variants: not selected; no new service, model download, silent XL upgrade or spend authorized.

No source, history, Music Lab capability or raw research is deleted. Research-run index preserves available originals/copies with exact provenance and lists missing assets. Future changes require a new ADR, not rewriting the prior decision.

## Revisit triggers

Low accepted-candidate yield; listeners reject the processed output; unacceptable queue latency/memory; live controls are not perceptually meaningful; stronger evidence or a completed, authorized competing-model comparison; or a requirement for guaranteed independent notes/stems. These are falsifiable reasons to revise the choice, not reasons to hide earlier options.
