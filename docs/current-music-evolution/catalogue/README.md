## Recommendation contract

Ask: How are you feeling? What is difficult right now? What would you like to change? Offer everyday goals such as focus, calm, feeling less overwhelmed, wind down or gentle lift. No diagnosis is required. Let people skip free text. Optional taste/exclusions help pick among starter concepts. Explain: Suggested because you asked for X and prefer Y. Best means a transparent preference-based fit, never a clinical optimum.

Offer Preview a starter -> Shape now, Try another style, or Create something new. Hide musical settings by default; Advanced explains requests versus direct DSP. Silence, skip and Stop remain first-class choices. Do not infer musical style from diagnosis, identity, age, culture or region. Source categories include populations and structural access problems, not just conditions.

## Eligibility and readiness

The adolescent pair is future-design coverage only, not available in the adult prototype. The crisis pair is optional accompaniment only after human-support-first handling, never recommended as crisis care. Other acute medical/withdrawal signals likewise override listening suggestions. No new screening or automated diagnosis is proposed. All 40 entries remain concept-only until separately generated, technically screened and auditioned. Reusing a reviewed musical asset across goals is allowed with explicit provenance; 40 names need not imply 40 unique audio files.

## Common behind-the-scenes recipe and QA

Four alternative palettes are taste options, not prescriptions: soft keys, sustained open strings, restrained acoustic, and beatless tones. Each pair deliberately offers contrasting melodic/rhythmic versus sustained textures. Ask about preference and sensory exclusions; allow a different palette. No required BPM/key/healing frequency. Bounded 30-90 second candidate generation is a calibration proposal; assemble user-selected 3/5/10/15 minute sessions through approved scheduling. Use confidence-aware analysis and human audition before ready status. No source-topic-specific efficacy score exists.
