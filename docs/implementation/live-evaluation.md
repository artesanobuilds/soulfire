## Required physical round trips

1. Submit fictional typed struggle; verify response differs appropriately across at least two journeys and is an actual provider response.
2. Dictate a fictional sentence, stop, edit the transcript, submit only after editing. Confirm microphone ends and private-field dictation does not send a companion request.
3. Start live voice with permission. Speak, hear an actual AI response, inspect transcripts; interrupt mid-speech, mute/unmute both mic and speaker, end and reconnect. Confirm context survives and no duplicate turns appear.
4. Switch to practice/quiet mid-session; verify actual microphone indicator disappears and playback stops. Test denial, disconnect, device removal, slow network and unsupported browser.
5. Test a real phone's software keyboard, background and screen-lock behavior. Desktop viewport emulation is not evidence of those behaviors.

Do not mark Proof of Concept One fully complete until actual text and voice round trips are evidenced. Keep the alternative-interface brainstorm separate and deferred until this gate is met or parent authorizes proceeding despite it.
